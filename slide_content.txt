05 · COMBAT FEEL | Melee Combat System | HOW A HIT LANDS | Swing fires a hitbox check in front of the player — radius + a forward cone so only what you’re facing is hit. | Each target is registered once per swing (de-duplication) so a single attack can’t multi-hit the same enemy. | Damage, range and swing speed are tuned per weapon, so a knife feels nothing like a Morgenstern. | Enemies react with hit + death animations and drop from the ENEMIES counter. | Player, enemy and weapon logic are driven by dedicated C# controllers (PlayerController, EnemyController, weapon + HUD scripts). | PRISM-7  ·  Weapon Trials | 06

--- SLIDE 9 ---
08 · NETWORKING | How Multiplayer Works — Photon | PRISM-7 networks players with Photon (PUN). One player acts as host / master client; everyone else connects into the same room. | ROOMS &amp; CODES | Create Room opens a session on Photon’s cloud and returns a short code. Join by Code drops a player straight into that room — up to 8 operators. | NETWORKED PLAYERS | Each player is a networked prefab with a PhotonView. Position, rotation and animation state are synced so everyone sees the same arena in real time. | HITS OVER THE NETWORK | Melee hits are sent as networked events / RPCs so damage and deaths register consistently on every client, not just the attacker’s screen. | MODES &amp; STATE | The host owns the match state — mode (Co-op / Hybrid / PvP), level, enemy spawns and scoring — and shares it with everyone in the room. | PRISM-7  ·  Weapon Trials | 09

09 · ENEMIES | AI &amp; Enemy Operators | ENEMY BEHAVIOUR | Enemies navigate the arena with Unity’s NavMesh — agents path around walls and crates toward the player. | A simple state machine drives them: idle / patrol → chase when the player is near → attack in range. | Counts and toughness scale per level, so later trials throw more operators at you. | Enemies share the melee, animation and damage systems used by the player for consistent combat. | Owned by Ali Alhawaj (AI &amp; Enemies). | PRISM-7  ·  Weapon Trials | 10

14 · ENGINEERING | Challenges &amp; How We Solved Them | Double / multi-hits | PROBLEM | One swing was hitting the same enemy several times. | FIX | De-duplicate targets per swing so each enemy is damaged once. | Hits landing behind the player | PROBLEM | Radius-only checks hit enemies you weren’t facing. | FIX | Added a forward cone so only what’s in front of the swing counts. | Animation retargeting | PROBLEM | Mixamo clips didn’t map cleanly onto our characters. | FIX | Imported as Humanoid and rebuilt the Animator transitions. | Enemy pathing | PROBLEM | Agents got stuck on arena geometry. | FIX | Re-baked the NavMesh and tuned agent radius / obstacles. | Network desync | PROBLEM | Hits / deaths didn’t match across players. | FIX | Sync transforms + send hits as networked events via Photon. | Git merge conflicts | PROBLEM | Five people editing the same Unity project. | FIX | Branch-per-feature workflow and disciplined merges. | PRISM-7  ·  Weapon Trials | 15
